# AI Visibility Index — 13 Indian Brands × 4 AI Engines

A dated research snapshot from the AI Search Visibility Testing Programme (runs: 2 November 2026). Non-branded buyer prompts were run across ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, with repeated runs per prompt. Full method: `METHODOLOGY.md`.

> AI answers vary by run, account and time. Figures below are mention rates across completed runs in this cycle. Coverage was partial for some brands and engines (free-tier limits truncated some legs); partial columns are labelled and were never treated as complete.

## How to read this

- A mention is not a recommendation; several brands appeared inside answers that recommended a competitor.
- Foundational gaps (missing llms.txt, commerce-only llms.txt, no answer-first content) recur across the losing group and are fixable at page level.
- The five winning brands are published here for the same reason as the gaps: the data is the data, in both directions.

*Per-company dossiers (prompt-level evidence, gap analysis and fix roadmaps) are working documents shared with the brands concerned, not published here. Point-of-contact details are never published.*
